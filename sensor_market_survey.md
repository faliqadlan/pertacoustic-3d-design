## Why Marine Recorders Fail in Oil Wells

| Failure Mode | Marine Spec | PHE Well Requirement | Gap |
|-------------|-------------|---------------------|-----|
| **Temperature** | 35–40°C max | 150°C continuous | 110°C shortfall |
| **Pressure** | 50–100 bar (500–1,000m depth) | 690 bar (10,000 PSI) | 590 bar shortfall |
| **Chemical** | Seawater (NaCl) | H₂S (6–25%), CO₂ (10%), crude oil | Sulfide Stress Cracking, seal degradation |
| **Piezo stability** | PZT-5A stable to ~80°C | Needs 150°C stability | Depolarization above Curie temp |
| **Seal materials** | Nitrile/EPDM O-rings | Need HNBR/FKM fluoroelastomers | Chemical incompatibility |
